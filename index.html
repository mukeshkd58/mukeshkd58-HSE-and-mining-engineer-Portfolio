About

I am an HSE professional with 4.5 years of field experience on a Greenfield underground coal gasification (UCG) project in Thar and on construction sites in Sindh, Pakistan. My work has centred on hazard identification and risk assessment, permit-to-work systems, gas monitoring, confined space controls, emergency preparedness, and ISO 45001 and ISO 14001 aligned management systems.

My background is in Mining Engineering (B.E., Mehran University of Engineering & Technology), and I am now studying Georesources and Geoenergy Engineering with a Sustainable Mining focus at Politecnico di Torino. I hold the NEBOSH International General Certificate and completed the HSE Engineering Specialization from Khalifa University on Coursera.

Alongside field safety work I am building skills in Python, SQL, Excel and Power BI, and I have published five portfolio projects on GitHub that explore how analytics and machine learning can support incident prevention. These are learning and portfolio projects built on public or synthetic data, not deployed operational systems.
